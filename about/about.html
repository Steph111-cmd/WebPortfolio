About me

Drummer | Guitarist | 3rd-Year IT Student


Hey there! I’m a drummer, guitarist, and an IT student, and I love blending creativity with technology. Music has always been a huge part of my life. Whether I’m behind the drum kit setting the groove or strumming a guitar to create melodies, I find joy in making music that connects with people. It’s more than just playing—it’s about feeling, expression, and storytelling.

At the same time, I’m in my third year of IT studies, diving deep into coding, web development, and problem-solving. I love how technology challenges me to think critically and come up with creative solutions. In a way, both music and tech require rhythm, precision, and passion—and that’s what keeps me going.

Always learning, always creating—let’s make something awesome together!
